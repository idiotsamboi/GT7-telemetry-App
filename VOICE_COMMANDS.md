## Hands-free operation

The microphone starts automatically when hands-free listening is enabled. Say a request and pause briefly; no button is needed. An optional Engineer prefix is accepted. Recognition pauses during spoken output and for 0.6 seconds afterward to avoid feedback, then resumes. Use Pause listening to switch to manual push-to-talk. Headphones help separate game audio from your voice. Speech mute and microphone listening are separate controls. Audio stays local and is not recorded.
